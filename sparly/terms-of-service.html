The service

Sparly is a personal and household budget tracker. You can enter expenses and income by hand and use AI receipt scanning, which reads the store, amount, date and category from a photo of a receipt. Budgeting features work without an account and keep your data on your device.

Accounts

AI scanning through our server is tied to an account so the monthly scan quota can be counted. You can register with an email and password. If you buy Sparly Pro without registering, we create an account for that purchase without an email; you can add an email later to use it on other devices. You are responsible for keeping your credentials safe. You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app (Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account).

Sparly Pro (subscription)

  • Sparly Pro is an auto-renewable subscription offered weekly or yearly. The current prices are shown in the app before you buy and are charged to your Apple ID.
  • New subscribers may get a free trial of the yearly plan (currently 3 days), as shown in the app. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged when it ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the same price and period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple ID is charged within 24 hours before the renewal.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription in your iPhone's Settings (your name → Subscriptions) or in the app (Settings → Manage subscription). Canceling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Payments and refunds are handled by Apple under its terms. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

Scan quota and fair use

AI scans are limited per calendar month because every scan costs us money. Sparly Pro includes up to 150 scans per month; during a free trial up to 10; without Pro, registered users get up to 10. The limit and its reset date are shown in the app. When you reach it you can still enter expenses by hand. Users who scan with their own OpenRouter API key are not limited by us.

Accuracy of AI results

AI reading of receipts can make mistakes (a wrong amount, date or category). Always check the data before saving. We do not guarantee the accuracy of automatically read data.

Opis usluge
