PushAlarm Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

PushAlarm is an alarm clock that turns off when you do push-ups in front of your phone's camera. It is made by Nedeljko Sikanjic, an individual developer in Austria ("we"). In short: PushAlarm does not collect, store on a server or share any personal data.

Camera

When the alarm rings, or when you use "Test camera", PushAlarm uses the front camera to see your arms and count push-ups. Every frame is analysed on your device with Apple's Vision framework and discarded right away. No photo or video is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The camera is only on while that screen is open.

Data stored on your device

Your alarm settings (time, repeat days, push-up target) and the dates of your finished alarms (for the streak) are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

PushAlarm schedules local notifications on your device as a backup in case the alarm sound is interrupted. They are not sent through any server.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Children

PushAlarm is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
