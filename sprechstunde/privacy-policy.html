Sprechstunde Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Sprechstunde helps you practise speaking German with an AI conversation partner. It is made by Nedeljko Sikanjic, an individual developer in Austria ("we"). This page explains what happens with your data.

Your voice

The app records your voice only while you hold the microphone button. The recording is sent once over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to an AI model to understand your answer, and is then deleted. We do not store recordings. The AI providers we use are selected so that they do not keep or train on this data ("zero data retention").

What is sent to the AI

Your spoken answer, the text of the practice situation and the conversation so far, your level and the language for explanations. We never send your name, email, contacts, location or an account number. In the practice situations you may use invented names and details.

Account

You do not need to sign up. The app creates an anonymous account from a random number stored in your iPhone's keychain. It lets us apply the daily practice limit and is not linked to your identity.

Stored on your device

Your goals, level, language, streak, finished situations and learned words are stored only on your iPhone.

Processors

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany): our server.
  • OpenRouter, Inc. (USA) and the model provider it routes to (currently Google): processing of answers. This may involve processing outside the EU.
  • Apple: purchases and subscriptions, if you buy Pro.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete my data. This deletes your anonymous account on our server and the data on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes the on-device data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data. Write to us at the address below.
